I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Machine Learning & Vision Lab, KAIST. I primarily work on audio inverse problems in domains such as room acoustics and audio effects. I received my Ph.D. from the Music and Audio Research Group, Seoul National University, and previously interned at Meta Reality Labs, Sony AI, and Supertone.
